HWP is the format of Hangul, the word processor most Korean schools, government offices and companies still use.
If you live or work in Korea, sooner or later someone sends you a form as a `.hwp` file — and your phone has nothing that can open it.

## Open it in the browser

The [HWP viewer & editor](../en) is a web page, not an app, so there is nothing to install. The file is not uploaded anywhere; it is opened on your phone.

**iPhone**

1. In Mail, KakaoTalk or Messages, long-press the attachment (or tap Share) and choose **Save to Files**.
2. Open the tool in Safari.
3. Tap the box that says "Drop an HWP, HWPX or Ichitaro file here". A file picker opens — choose the file you just saved.

**Android**

1. Download the attachment. It usually lands in **Files → Downloads**.
2. Open the tool in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
3. Tap the drop box and pick the downloaded `.hwp`.

Once it opens, the tables and text appear on screen, and the fields that were empty in the original are highlighted in yellow.

## Fill it in and send it back

1. Tap a yellow field and type. You can also change text that is already there, and tick checkboxes (□ → ■).
2. Tap **Save as HWP**. The file is saved as `originalname_filled.hwp`.
3. Find it in Files → Downloads (iPhone) or My Files → Downloads (Android) and send it back.

The saved file keeps the original structure and only replaces the fields you changed, so whoever receives it sees their own form, laid out as they made it, when they open it in Hangul.

## Good to know

- **The on-screen layout is an approximation.** Line breaks may fall a little differently from the original. The saved file still opens with its original layout.
- Forms with many cells are small on a phone screen — pinch to zoom.
- If you only need to *read* the file, Hancom's own viewer app or the document viewer in the Naver Whale browser will also open it. They are for reading, though; to fill a form in and send it back, you need an editor.
- Password-protected or DRM-locked documents will not open.

Files ending in **.hwpx** open the same way — see [what is an HWPX file](hwpx).
