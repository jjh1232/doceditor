Korean application and consent forms often have a "서명 또는 인" box — *signature or seal*.
The usual routine is print → sign with a pen → scan or photograph → turn it back into a file. Without a printer, that is where it stops.

With the [HWP viewer & editor](../en) you draw the signature on screen and drop it into the box.

## Add a handwritten signature

1. Open the `.hwp` file.
2. In the **Signature** panel on the right, draw your signature in the box with a mouse, trackpad or finger.
3. A signature tile appears underneath. **Drag it onto the signature box**, or pick it and then click where it should go.
4. Drag a corner to resize it.
5. Click **Save as HWP**.

The signature goes in as a **transparent image**, so you can place it over the "(서명)" or "(인)" text and no white rectangle covers the words.
On a phone, drawing with your finger looks close to a pen signature.

## Add an ID photo

1. In the **Photo** panel, choose a photo or drag a photo file straight in.
2. Click the photo box on the form, or drop the photo there. A table cell or a line outside a table both work.
3. Drag a corner to fit it to the box.

Korean résumé photo boxes are usually 3×4 cm. To crop your photo to that size first,
use the [ID photo size fitter](/idphoto/en) and pick the résumé size — it then fits the box cleanly.

## Removing a picture that is already there

If the form has a sample photo or a "paste photo here" graphic, click **Remove** on it. If you remove the wrong one, click **Undo**.

## Good to know

- Signatures and photos work in **HWP files only**. HWPX files support text for now.
- HWP edits are written back into the original file's space, so several large photos can trigger "The new content doesn’t fit in the space the original file left". Use a smaller photo, or fewer, and it saves.
- This is an image of your handwritten signature, not a legally certified digital signature. Check that the recipient accepts a signature image.
- Your signature and photo are not uploaded. They go into the file inside your browser.
